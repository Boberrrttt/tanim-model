# Mode of Inorganic Fertilizer Application

| Crop Planted | Mode of Inorganic Fertilizer Application |
| :--- | :--- |
| **ABACA** | **EP Once / NT Once**<br>• **At Planting:** Apply the fertilizer 7.5-10 cm below and 10-15 cm to the side of the suckers.<br>• **Established Plant:** Apply the fertilizer in 5-6 holes bored 5-6 cm deep around the plants right after harvest. This may be repeated every four (4) months. |
| **ALFALFA** | • **1st Application:** N + 2/3(P+K). Broadcast the fertilizer.<br>• **2nd Application:** 1/3(P+K). Topdress the fertilizer for maintenance. |
| **ALGAE** | • **1st Application:** 1/2 N + (P+K). Drain the pond and allow to dry for 2-3 weeks. Broadcast the fertilizer and fill the pond with water to a depth of 10 cm.<br>• **2nd Application:** N. Broadcast the fertilizer after one (1) month and increase the water to a depth of 25-30 cm as the algae/lablab grow thicker. |
| **ALUGBATI** | • **1st Application:** 1/3 N + (P+K). Apply the fertilizer 8 cm to the side of the growing plant a week after cultivation. Water after application: Urea (45-0-0) = 1 tablespoon per hill.<br>• **2nd Application:** 2/3 N. Apply the fertilizer after the first harvest. Water after application.<br>• **3rd Application:** 1/3 N. Apply the fertilizer every month. |
| **AMPALAYA** | **(N+P+K)/2** <br>• **1st Application:** Mix the fertilizer with the soil at planting time.<br>• **2nd Application:** Sidedress the fertilizer when the first set of fruit begin to form. |
| **APPLE** | • **1st Application:** P + K. Apply the fertilizer during the resting period.<br>• **2nd Application:** 2/3 N. Apply the fertilizer before the rainy season.<br>• **3rd Application:** 1/3 N. Apply the fertilizer during summer. |
| **ASPARAGUS** | • **1st Application:** P + K. Broadcast the fertilizer before planting and work into the soil to a depth of 7.5 cm.<br>• **2nd Application:** N. Scatter the fertilizer along the rows at intervals of about 14 days during the cutting season. |
| **ATIS** | **(Once)** Work the fertilizer into the soil in small doses. Repeat this process during the first few years. |
| **AVOCADO** | **(Once)** Work the fertilizer into the soil in small doses. Repeat this process during the first few years. |
| **BANANA** | **N + P + K** <br>Apply the fertilizer in four (4) equal doses during the following periods: <br>• **1st Application:** Two months after planting.<br>• **2nd Application:** Fifth month after planting.<br>• **3rd Application:** Eight months after planting.<br>• **4th Application:** Eleven months after planting before flowering stage. |
| **BAGUIO BEANS** | **ONCE**<br>Apply the fertilizer in bands 5-7.5 cm to the side and 2.5-5 cm below the level of the seed. |
| **BATAO** | **NT: Once**<br>Apply the fertilizer in bands 5.0-7.5 cm to the side and 2.5-5 cm below the level of the seeds, or apply the fertilizer as soon as the vines reach a height of one (1) meter. |
| **BELL PEPPER** | • **1st Application:** Apply the fertilizer including 2/3 of the N during the last operation in the preparation of the land, preferably along the line of the rows.<br>• **2nd Application:** Sidedress with the remaining N fertilizer when the first set of fruits appear. |
| **BLACK PEPPER** | • **1st Application:** Apply the fertilizer including 2/3 of the N during the last operation in the preparation of the land, preferably along the line of the rows.<br>• **2nd Application:** Sidedress with the remaining N fertilizer when the first set of fruits appear. |
| **BROCCOLI** | • **1st Application:** 1/3 N + (P+K). Apply the fertilizer at the base of the plant after transplanting.<br>• **2nd Application:** 2/3 N. Sidedress the fertilizer six (6) weeks after transplanting. |
| **BUSH SITAO** | • **1st Application:** (1/2 N + P + K). Broadcast/incorporate the fertilizer by tillage either at the time of sowing or soon after the plants come up.<br>• **2nd Application:** (1/2 N). Topdress the fertilizer after flowering but not during the first blooming. |
| **CABBAGE** | • **1st Application:** 1/2 N + (P+K). Apply the fertilizer as a double band 10-12.5 cm on both sides of the row at 10 cm deep at planting.<br>• **2nd Application:** (1/2 N). Sidedress the fertilizer 10-14 days after planting and/or a part of this may be applied two (2) weeks before harvest. |
| **CACAO** | **(Once) NT: (N+P+K)/2** <br>• **Seedling (EP):** Mix the fertilizer with the topsoil, replace into the bored holes 30-50 cm deep and transplant the seedling over it.<br>• **Established Crop:**<br>  - **1st Application:** Apply the fertilizer at the start of the rainy season.<br>  - **2nd Application:** Apply the fertilizer before the end of the rainy season. |
| **CAIMITO (STAR APPLE)** | **(Once) NT / EP: (N+P+K)/2** <br>• **At Planting:** Apply the fertilizer 10-12.5 cm deep and 7.5 cm to the side of the plant.<br>• **Established Crop:**<br>  - **1st Application:** Apply the fertilizer in borings 12.5-15 cm deep at the start of the rainy season.<br>  - **2nd Application:** Apply the fertilizer before the end of the rainy season. |
| **CALAMANSI** | • **At Planting:** Incorporate the fertilizer with the soil to a depth of 15-25 cm before planting.<br>• **1st Application:** 1/3 N + (P+K). Apply the fertilizer at the start of the rainy season or when irrigation is possible, at the flowering bud initiation.<br>• **2nd Application:** 2/3 N. Apply the fertilizer at the end of the rainy season.<br>*Note: Fertilizer should be applied in borings around the plant 5-7.5 cm deep and outside half the area shaded by the canopy.* |
| **CAMOTE** | **ONCE**<br>Apply the fertilizer in a band 7.5-12.5 cm to the side along the row at a depth of 10-15 cm at transplanting. |
| **CARROTS** | • **1st Application:** 1/2 N + (P+K). Broadcast the fertilizer and incorporate into the soil to about 10 cm deep just before seeding.<br>• **2nd Application:** 1/2 N. Topdress after seedlings have taken roots or 30 days after sowing. |
| **CASSAVA** | • **1st Application:** 1/2 N + (P+K). Apply the fertilizer in the furrow during planting.<br>• **2nd Application:** (1/2 N). Sidedress the fertilizer 2-3 months after planting during hilling up. |
| **CASHEW** | **(N+P+K)/2** <br>• **1st Application:** Apply the fertilizer at the start of the rainy season.<br>• **2nd Application:** Apply the fertilizer before the end of the rainy season. |
| **CASTOR OIL BEAN** | **ONCE**<br>Apply the fertilizer 7.5 cm to the side, 5-7.5 cm deep in the row at planting. |
| **CAULIFLOWER** | • **1st Application:** 1/2 N + (P+K). Apply the fertilizer as a double band 10-12.5 cm on both sides of the row at 10 cm deep at planting.<br>• **2nd Application:** (1/2 N). Sidedress the fertilizer 10-14 days after planting and/or a part of this may be applied two (2) weeks before harvest. |
| **CELERY** | **(N+P+K)/2** <br>• **1st Application:** Broadcast the fertilizer and plow into the soil during land preparation.<br>• **2nd Application:** Sidedress the fertilizer during the vegetative period. |
| **CHAYOTE** | • **1st Application:** 2/3 N + (P+K). Mix the fertilizer with the soil at planting time.<br>• **2nd Application:** 1/3 N. Sidedress the fertilizer when fruits begin to form. |
| **CHICO** | **ONCE**<br>• **At Planting:** Apply the fertilizer 7.5-10 cm below and to the side of the plant.<br>• **Established Plant:** Apply the fertilizer uniformly around the base or in borings 7.5-10 cm deep at outside half the area shaded by the canopy. |
| **CHILI / PEPPER (Siling Labuyo)** | • **1st Application:** N + 2/3 (P + 1/2 K). Apply the fertilizer including 2/3 of the N during the last operation in the preparation of the land, preferably along the line of the rows.<br>• **2nd Application:** 1/3 N + (1/2 K). Sidedress with the remaining N fertilizer when the first set of fruits appear. |
| **CHINESE CABBAGE** | • **1st Application:** 1/2 N + (P+K). Apply the fertilizer as a dressing 8-14 days before planting.<br>• **2nd Application:** 1/2 N. Topdress the fertilizer 2-5 weeks after planting. |
| **CITRUS** | • **At Planting:** Incorporate the fertilizer with the soil to a depth of 15-25 cm before planting.<br>• **1st Application:** 1/3 N + (P+K). Apply the fertilizer at the start of the rainy season or when irrigation is possible, at the flowering bud initiation.<br>• **2nd Application:** 2/3 N. Apply the fertilizer at the end of the rainy season.<br>*Note: Fertilizer should be applied in borings around the plant 5-7.5 cm deep and outside half the area shaded by the canopy.* |
| **CITRONELLA** | • **1st Application:** (P+K). Broadcast P and K fertilizers before growth starts.<br>• **2nd Application:** (1/2 N). Topdress with half N fertilizer once growth starts.<br>• **3rd Application:** (1/2 N). Topdress with remaining N fertilizer 6-8 weeks after the 2nd application. |
| **COCONUT PALM TREE** | • **New Transplant (Once):** Apply the fertilizer in the hole, cover with a little topsoil, and transplant the seedling over it preferably at the start of the rainy season.<br>• **Established Plant (Once):** Apply the fertilizer every six (6) months for a period of two (2) years, after which they are made annually, at the start of the rainy season & towards the end of the rainy season. Application is made in at least 10 holes 20-40 cm deep and bored 1-2 meters from the base of the tree. |
| **COFFEE** | **Seedling (Once) / EP: (N+P+K)/2** <br>• **Seedling:** Incorporate the fertilizer with soil to a depth of 15-25 cm before planting.<br>• **1st Application:** Apply the fertilizer at the start of the rainy season.<br>• **2nd Application:** Apply the fertilizer before the end of the rainy season. |
| **CORN / HYBRID CORN** | • **1st Application:** 1/2 N + (P+K). Apply the fertilizer in a band 5.0 cm to the side and 5.0 cm below the seed at planting.<br>• **2nd Application:** 1/2 N. Sidedress the fertilizer when the plants are about four (4) weeks old or about knee high. |
| **COTTON** | • **1st Application:** 2/3 N + (P+K). Apply the fertilizer in a band 5 cm to the sides of and 5-10 cm deep at sowing time.<br>• **2nd Application:** 1/3 N. Sidedress the fertilizer to the plants before flowering. |
| **COWPEA** | **NT: Once**<br>Apply the fertilizer in bands 5.0-7.5 cm to the side and 2.5-5 cm below the level of the seeds, or apply the fertilizer as soon as the vines reach a height of one (1) meter. |

| **SITAO** | • **1st Application:** (1/2 N + P + K). Broadcast/incorporate the fertilizer by tillage either at the time of sowing or soon after the plants come up.<br>• **2nd Application:** (1/2 N). Topdress the fertilizer after flowering but not during the first blooming. |
| **SINGKAMAS** | • **1st Application:** 1/2 N + (P + 1/2 K). Apply the fertilizer in the furrow 7 cm to the side and 7 cm below the seedling at planting.<br>• **2nd Application:** 1/2 N + 1/2 K. Sidedress the fertilizer before hilling up, 2 months. |
| **SISAL** | **(ONCE)**<br>Same as young tree.<br>Broadcast or distribute in band in the neighborhood of the plant or distribute uniformly over the area shaded by the plant leaves the fertilizer at the start of the rainy season. |
| **SNAP BEANS** | **(ONCE)**<br>Apply the fertilizer in bands 5-7.5 cm to the side and 2.5-5 cm below the level of the seed. |
| **SPINACH** | • **1st Application:** 3/4 N + (P+K). Apply the fertilizer at transplanting time.<br>• **2nd Application:** 1/4 N. Sidedress the fertilizer two (2) weeks before harvest. |
| **SPRING ONION** | • **1st Application:** (1/2 N + P + K). Apply the fertilizer as a double band 10-12.5 cm on both sides of the row at 10 cm deep at planting.<br>• **2nd Application:** (1/2 N). Sidedress the fertilizer 10-14 days after planting and/or a part of this may be applied two (2) weeks before harvest. |
| **SOY BEAN** | **NT: Once**<br>Apply the fertilizer in bands 5.0-7.5 cm to the side and 2.5-5 cm below the level of the seeds, or apply the fertilizer as soon as the vines reach a height of one (1) meter. |
| **SQUASH** | • **1st Application:** 1/2 N + (P+K). Mix the fertilizer with the soil at planting time.<br>• **2nd Application:** 1/2 N. Sidedress the fertilizer when fruits begin to form. |
| **STRAWBERRY** | Apply the fertilizer in two (2) equal doses: <br>• **1st application:** Basal at planting or at the start of the rainy season.<br>• **2nd application:** 2 months after planting or before the end of the rainy season. |
| **STRING BEANS / STRING SITAO** | • **1st Application:** (1/2 N + P + K). Broadcast/incorporate the fertilizer by tillage either at the time of sowing or soon after the plants come up. Alternatively, apply the fertilizer in a band 7.5 cm alongside the row at a depth of 7.5-10 cm either at sowing or soon after they come up with a heavy set of pods.<br>• **2nd Application:** (1/2 N). Topdress the fertilizer after flowering but not during the first blooming. |
| **SUGARCANE** | • **1st Application:** 1/2 N + P + 1/2 K. Apply the fertilizer on either one side or both sides of the row.<br>• **2nd Application:** 1/2 N + 1/2 K. Sidedress the fertilizer 2-3 months after the first application.<br>*Note: For ratoon cropping, follow the same procedure as that of plant cane crop.* |
| **SUNFLOWER** | Apply the fertilizer bi-weekly; water the plants daily. |
| **SWEET CORN** | • **1st Application:** (P+K). Broadcast P and K fertilizers before growth starts.<br>• **2nd Application:** (1/2 N). Topdress with half N fertilizer once growth starts.<br>• **3rd Application:** (1/2 N). Topdress with remaining N fertilizer 6-8 weeks after the 2nd application. |
| **SWEET PEPPER** | • **1st Application:** 2/3 N + P + 1/2 K. Apply the fertilizer during the last operation in the land preparation, preferably along the row.<br>• **2nd Application:** 1/3 N + 1/2 K. Sidedress the fertilizer when first set of fruits appear. |
| **TAMARIND** | **1/2 N + (P+K)**<br>Apply fertilizer at the start of the rainy season and before end of the rainy season. |
| **TAPILAN** | **NT: Once**<br>Apply the fertilizer in bands 5.0-7.5 cm to the side and 2.5-5 cm below the level of the seeds, or apply the fertilizer as soon as the vines reach a height of one (1) meter. |
| **TANGANTANGAN** | **(ONCE)**<br>Apply the fertilizer 7.5 cm to the side, 5-7.5 cm deep in the row at planting. |
| **TANGLAD** | • **1st Application:** (P+K). Broadcast P and K fertilizers before growth starts.<br>• **2nd Application:** (1/2 N). Topdress with half N fertilizer once growth starts.<br>• **3rd Application:** (1/2 N). Topdress with remaining N fertilizer 6-8 weeks after the 2nd application. |
| **TIESSA** | Work the fertilizer into the soil in small doses. Repeat this process during the first few years. |
| **TOBACCO** | • **1st Application:** 1/2 N + (P+K). Apply the fertilizer 7 cm to the side and 5 cm below the plant at transplanting.<br>• **2nd Application:** 1/2 N. Dissolve the fertilizer in water and apply 7-10 days after transplanting.<br>*Note: Avoid using fertilizer that contains chlorides.* |
| **TOMATO** | • **1st Application:** N + (P+K). Apply the fertilizer 7.0 cm away from the base of the plant and 7.0 cm deeper than the root system before or at planting.<br>• **2nd Application:** 1/2 N. Sidedress the fertilizer when the plants start to bloom or when there are small fruits developing. Repeat after every two weeks.<br>*Note: Tomatoes are sensitive to too heavy application of N and may result in delayed ripening of fruits that are soft and split easily.* |
| **TUGI / YAM** | • **1st Application:** 1/2 N + (P+K). Apply the fertilizer in a band 7-12 cm to the side and 7 cm deep at transplanting.<br>• **2nd Application:** 1/2 N. Sidedress the fertilizer during hilling up or a month after transplanting. |
| **TURNIP** | • **1st Application:** 1/2 N + (P + 1/2 K). Apply the fertilizer in the furrow 7 cm to the side and 7 cm below the seedling at planting.<br>• **2nd Application:** 1/2 N + 1/2 K. Sidedress the fertilizer before hilling up, 2 months. |
| **UBE** | • **1st Application:** 1/2 N + (P+K). Apply the fertilizer in a band 7-12 cm to the side and 7 cm deep at transplanting.<br>• **2nd Application:** 1/2 N. Sidedress the fertilizer during hilling up or a month after transplanting. |
| **UPO** | **N + P + K/2** <br>• **1st Application:** Uniformly mix the fertilizer with soil at planting.<br>• **2nd Application:** Sidedress the fertilizer when fruits begin to form. |
| **WATERMELON** | • **1st Application:** 1/2 N + (P+K). Apply the fertilizer in trenches near the row or in a strip where the row is to be located, during planting time.<br>• **2nd Application:** 1/2 N. Sidedress the fertilizer when vines begin to run and again when fruits are half grown.<br>*Note: Watermelon are sensitive to chlorides.* |
| **WINGED, SEGUIDELLAS (BEANS)** | • **1st Application:** 1/2 N + (P+K). Apply the fertilizer in a band 7.5 cm alongside the row at a depth of 7.5-10 cm either at sowing or soon after they come up with a heavy set of pods.<br>• **2nd Application:** 1/2 N. Topdress the fertilizer after flowering. |
| **YACON** | **(ONCE)**<br>Apply the fertilizer in bands 7.5-12.5 cm to the side along the row at a depth of 10-15 cm at transplanting. |
